RunSGD Beta

Privacy & Beta Notice

Last updated 19 September 2026. RunSGD is an early beta companion for Singapore ↔ Johor commuters.

What RunSGD stores

If you create an account, RunSGD stores your account identifier, display name, optional profile photo and bio, membership tag, community messages, beta feedback, and Journey activity you choose to start. Saved Journey activity can include origin area, destination, travel request, interpreted travel preferences, route type, transport-mode tags, status and timestamps. Detailed driving polylines are not stored in Journey history. Your chosen Home widget layout and an active journey may also be stored locally in your browser.

Location

Location is used only when you allow it, for features such as nearby prayer places, local air quality, route planning and live journey guidance. A saved Journey may keep the origin area and selected destination so recent trips can be shown again. RunSGD does not ask you to enter your exact home address.

AI & Journey personalization

When you use RunSGD AI, relevant app context may be sent through the RunSGD backend to Google Gemini to generate a short answer or interpret how you want to travel. Journey Planner requires sign-in. RunSGD can use your own recent Journey tags to show a commute profile and make repeated planning easier; your current request remains the primary input. You can clear your saved Journey history from More → Journey personalization. External web search is currently disabled. API keys are stored server-side and are not exposed to normal users.

Community

Community posts are visible to other RunSGD users and guests. Do not post private personal information, passwords, exact home addresses, or sensitive details about other people.

Third-party data

RunSGD may display information from services such as LTA DataMall, Open-Meteo, exchange-rate providers, Google News/GDELT, OpenStreetMap and other configured services. Availability and accuracy can vary.

Beta limitations

Traffic, weather, FX, community and AI outputs are informational and may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Use official sources and your own judgement for immigration, legal, financial, safety or travel decisions.
