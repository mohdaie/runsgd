Ask RunSGD AI
Can I use it from either side of the border?+

Can lah. RunSGD is for people in Singapore and Johor: work in Tanjong Pagar, shop in JB, or just head out for makan. Choose the country and destination in the app.

Is RunSGD really free?+

All current features are free during this limited-time offer. AI journey planning and saved history require sign-in. Premium Android is coming soon; its features and pricing have not been announced.

Do I need to download anything?+

Open RunSGD in your browser and start there. You can also add it to your home screen for the app experience. Premium Android is still coming soon.

What do the traffic and ERP features show?+

Driving navigation uses available Google traffic coverage in Singapore and Johor for traffic-aware arrival estimates and route checks. ERP warnings show route-based gantry locations in Singapore. They do not show rates or current charging status.

Does the arrival time include the checkpoint queue?+

Checkpoint waiting and immigration processing times are not included. Check the Causeway snapshot before you leave and allow a buffer for border formalities.

Where can I ask questions or send feedback?+

Head to Explore to ask RunSGD AI about your trip. For an app issue or suggestion, use Feedback in the More tab.
