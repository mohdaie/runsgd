Terms of use

RunSGD is a beta travel companion for Singapore and Johor. These notes describe how to use its current features.

Travel information

Routes, arrival estimates, traffic, exchange rates, service status and AI answers depend on available data and may be incomplete or change. Check official immigration and transport sources for travel requirements. Checkpoint waiting and border processing times are not included in journey estimates.

Singapore ERP warnings identify gantry locations along a route. They do not confirm charging status, operating hours or the rate you will pay. Follow road signs and applicable rules, and operate your device safely.

Accounts and use

AI journey planning and saved history require sign-in. Keep your account secure. Use the service responsibly and do not attempt to access another person's account or private data.

Current offer

All current features are free for a limited time. Premium Android is coming soon. Its features and pricing have not been announced.

Privacy and feedback

Read the privacy and beta notice for details about location, account data and AI use. Send app issues or suggestions through Feedback in the app's More tab.
