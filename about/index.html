How it works

Plan · Choose · Go

Your start point is always where you are. From there, it takes three steps.

1

Plan it in plain words

Pick a destination and describe the trip the way you'd say it.

"I have a car, but park in JB if it makes sense. KTM is okay if it's easier."
"I'm riding a motorcycle."
2

Choose your route

RunSGD shows options that fit what you asked for. Each option is split into clear legs ("hops").

🚇 MRT & bus🚌 Cross-border bus🚗 Driving🏍️ Motorcycle🚆 KTM Shuttle Tebrau
3

Let it guide you

Start the trip and RunSGD follows your GPS step by step. Walking legs use a heading-up map that turns with you. Tick off each hop as you finish it. If plans change, tap ↻ Reroute from here.

Honest about the border

We tell you what we know, and say what we don't.

Checkpoint queues, border bus waits and parking times aren't published live anywhere. RunSGD won't make up a door-to-door ETA for a cross-border trip. It shows the known legs with real times and marks immigration, border buses and transfers as their own steps. You can see exactly where the uncertainty is.

  • Woodlands Checkpoint ↔ Kranji MRT (170X / 170) and ↔ Marsiling / Woodlands MRT (950) are built in
  • Onward Singapore and Johor legs are routed with live transit and road data
  • KTM Shuttle Tebrau is shown as its own clearly labelled option

FAQ

Good questions.

Is RunSGD free?

Yes. It's in open beta.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open runsgd.site in your phone browser. To use it like an app, choose "Add to Home Screen".

Does it work in Johor as well as Singapore?

Yes. Search, weather, prayer times and routing cover both sides. Live nearby bus arrivals are Singapore-only for now and turn on automatically once you're in Singapore.

Can it tell me how long the checkpoint queue is?

Not yet, because no reliable live source exists. RunSGD shows the live Causeway camera snapshots and gives accurate times for every leg it can measure.

Is the information official?

No. RunSGD pulls from public sources such as LTA DataMall and OneMap. Always check official sources for immigration, legal and safety decisions.

Found a bug or have an idea?
